# Sprint 4 terminal guard handoff

**Reuse the exact predecessor guard through a separately approved handoff.** Keep the guard at `src/.s4-maintenance-package` until the successor reaches its existing held package exchange. Give the successor a fresh private capsule, maintenance ID, candidate, attempt directory, alternate exchange generation, and installer transaction.

## Why reuse is smaller

The predecessor's terminal verifier opens the guard at its named parked path and matches its inode and mount to the controller record and installer receipt. The operation that parks the package already accepts a verified marker directory at that name. Publication returns the same guard inode to the parked name. The current blockers are the default absence checks when creating and enrolling a fresh attempt. [Terminal checks](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/controller.py:980) · [Installer receipt verification](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/controller.py:733) · [Gate exchange](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/gate.py:75) · [Fresh controller](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/controller.py:574) · [Preinstall boundary](/tmp/jsh-s4-preinstall-abort-repair.KmZxSmEx/preinstall_abort.py:124)

| Approach | Effect on evidence and recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Same-mount archival move | A same-mount no-replace rename preserves the inode and frees the operational path. It immediately breaks the predecessor's current terminal verifier, which opens the named parked path. Preserving readable historical proof would require a separate archive-aware verifier, an approved custody plan, and replay states for the source and archive locations. With distinct parent directories, both need fsync. A crash after removing the guard also creates a distinct handoff state the present controller cannot interpret. |
| Explicit predecessor guard reuse | Keeps the original guard at the exact name the terminal verifier expects through enrollment. The existing atomic exchange moves it to the canonical name only during the successor hold and returns it at publication. The old receipt and installer journal remain byte-identical. They remain historical evidence during the successor operation; the old live terminal verifier is expected to hold whenever the canonical source or placement differs. |

## Private authority and recovery

1. Issue one canonical, independently pinned `predecessor-guard.json` for the exact successor capsule. While the predecessor is still in its restored layout, verify its approved tools and terminal `RELEASED/ROLLED_BACK` record, actual `ROLLED_BACK` installer transaction and matching receipt, original canonical source and named guard, no pending runtime hold, and the terminal restored alternate exchange with its exact proof. Bind the project, predecessor capsule and hold ID, controller journal, full installer transaction and receipt digests, canonical package identity, source manifest and protected pin, and the restored exchange identity and signed-off terminal proof. Also bind the successor's physical capsule, new hold ID, approved checker and manifest, and newly prepared alternate exchange policy and intent. Bind the guard parent and directory identities and mount, exact permissions/owner, and the sole `__init__.py` file's exact inode, safe link count, owner, mode, and marker bytes/digest. Store the file privately and durably alongside the successor plan. A repeated issue accepts only identical evidence for that successor.
2. Use the predecessor's existing terminal verifier on issuance under its lease protocol. At successor enrollment and start, verify the authoritative handoff digest and reread its filesystem evidence, original pinned journal/receipt, current guard, and canonical source with the appropriate existing locks. Once the fresh alternate exchange parks the same alternate paths, the predecessor's restored exchange journal remains historical proof. The active fence must then pass the successor's independent closed-exchange checks. Calling the old live restored exchange witness after the new fence closes would reject the deliberately changed placement. Use the common project install gate to serialize attempts and avoid reacquiring shared locks already held by the current process. The successor still performs its own fresh protected-state, service, process and launch attestations. [Predecessor terminal leases](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/controller.py:989) · [Successor run and lease order](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/controller.py:1009) · [Live exchange placement check](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/host_exchange.py:461)
3. Persist the inherited guard identity in the successor's durable `CLOSING` record before any hold exchange. Before publishing that record, the guard is in the predecessor's original placement and recovery can repeat exact validation. After the record exists, validate one of the exact canonical/parked inode pairs dictated by phase. On a crash before the `HELD` or `PUBLISHED` journal update, either the move has not happened or the exact atomic exchange already happened. Reread the pair, fsync the parent, and let the existing controller replay advance only its own journal. A missing or replaced inherited object holds. Omit any fallback to marker creation when an inherited guard is expected. [Held transition](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/controller.py:688) · [Publication replay](/tmp/jsh-s4-enrollment-release.EHxLVsCF/controller.py:824)

The handoff is scoped to one approved successor tuple. The existing project lease and before-image checks serialize use. A separate global predecessor state is unnecessary, and an early untouched refusal can still be followed by a separately authorized fresh successor.

## File-level implementation boundary

- **Successor `plan.json` and `predecessor-guard.json`.** Add one optional approved exact path/digest pointer and bind it through the normal controller tool/file binding. The handoff pins the successor tuple directly, so it need not reference a plan digest that would include its own digest. Use existing privacy, canonical encoding and SHA-256 conventions. The default plan still requires an absent parked guard.
- **Successor `controller.py`.** Add a common preinstall parked-boundary verifier. It supplies the exact expected guard only for the approved handoff, validates the canonical before-image and the predecessor's authenticated immutable evidence as appropriate, and seeds `CLOSING.guard`. Preserve the existing independent manifest, candidate, source, marker, unit, installer and writer lease checks. Reread the pinned placement before and after exchanges. Close guard validation descriptors before invoking existing host attestations so the process census remains exact. Route only the successor's own journal and installer directory to the installer.
- **Successor `gate.py`.** Use an expected-guard parameter for the pinned inherited branch at the existing park, publish and rehold calls. Prove the inherited guard at the expected name before each operation and verify the exact post-exchange pair. Keep the ordinary first-release path and its marker creation behavior. Do not add a third physical package path or an alternate import route.
- **Successor `preinstall_abort.py`.** Replace only the blanket parked-path absence clause with the shared exact check. Commit the optional authority and exact references to the enrollment through the controller binding, and revalidate at enrollment, capture, a first-preflight refusal, and the existing abort-restoration boundary. Keep all other no-start checks and exact refusal semantics. A progressed transaction still follows normal rollback; it does not qualify for the preinstall path.
- **Existing boundaries.** Keep the predecessor capsule and all of its receipt, controller, installer and restoration files unchanged. The successor installer, receipt schema, normal launcher/bootstrap, host-check package routes, alternate exchange mechanism and application/SQLite/Sheets data paths can keep their present contracts. Preparing the handoff writes only new private successor evidence. The successor's later authorized release continues to be the existing source transaction. ## Proving tests

- First release without a handoff remains valid with an absent parked path. A plausible marker without authority, a mismatched or nonterminal predecessor, a missing or changed installer receipt, a reused transaction directory, or a handoff for a different successor must hold before any exchange.
- Replace the guard with equal bytes at another inode, a wrong mount, symlink, wrong mode, wrong owner, extended directory, altered marker inode, or extra hard link. Also substitute the exact approval, protected pin, checker, manifest, or alternate exchange. Every such change must hold. Race replacement between park precheck and exchange/replay and verify the controller never issues a successful hold or release with the wrong pair.
- Inject interruption before and after the durable handoff write, after the `CLOSING` write, after atomic park and before fsync or `HELD`, and after atomic publish before `PUBLISHED`. Replay the same authority and check exact before/after placement and no second swap or substitute marker. Repeat for a successor rollback and rejection of foreign combinations.
- Run consecutive disposable releases through predecessor terminal rollback and successor install/rollback. Check a distinct controller/installer journal, hold ID, and receipt for the successor, the same original guard inode, byte-identical prior receipts and restore evidence, unchanged application/job-state pins during handoff, and the existing held and admitted launcher checks. Test that restored predecessor exchange evidence remains verifiable historically while the fresh exact exchange is closed, and that pre-invocation and exact first-preflight-abort paths work with the inherited guard without treating a progressed stop as early refusal.
