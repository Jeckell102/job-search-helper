**HOLD at the source gate.** The selected retry command can spend its sole budget after a projected workbook source change or retry veto. Ordinary Company-event replay can archive and consume the queue after an interrupted start that never persisted Company membership.

## Blocking reproductions

1. **Workbook read omits the ATS and retry controls.** The command requests only Company ID, Company Status and careers URL. The compact Companies projection also exposes ATS / Source and Automatic Retry?. The admission checker compares ATS only when present and treats a missing retry flag as allowed. In separate exact signed failure fixtures, I left SQLite untouched and changed the selected workbook row first to a different ATS and then, in a fresh fixture, to Automatic Retry? = No. Both calls returned ADMITTED_LOCAL_CYCLE, moved the ledger to CLAIMED, and replayed the same admitted successor. No provider or Sheets write was needed to reproduce the problem. Include both projected fields in the exact row proof and require their presence. Reject a source mismatch or retry veto before the claim, preserving the SCHEDULED ledger and absence of a successor. ## Verified boundaries

- The actual operator command held the unfinished historical State and then a mirror-only unfinished copy before constructing a workbook reader. Once both copies were terminal, it held the cooldown before constructing the workbook reader. A substituted outcome, runtime pin, approval scope and archived event revision were rejected before workbook access. The suite also verified that an A-to-B-to-A local source change cannot claim the earlier event.
- Scheduling accepted one outcome then replayed with no new delivery. Existing tests covered lost sender acknowledgement and uncertain claim. In the admission helper, a durable claim with no State stayed held, State without the mirror stayed partial, and mirror without Company membership stayed partial. A complete checkpoint replayed its exact saved cycle without starting another. Positive controls checked one selected Company, inherited runtime, pinned request fingerprint, and one retry index/cap.
- Operator retry admission used only a read-only workbook factory, never invoked the provider beyond the prerequisite stubbed original event, and produced zero workbook batches during scheduling or admission. It did not use global synchronization or a public successor queue. Active successor execution and live verification were outside this review.
- The ordinary happy path archived the verified request under its digest and preserved it across replacement of the mutable last-request file. The interrupted membership path is the exception shown above.
