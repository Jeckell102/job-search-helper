Its docstring explicitly says there is no production caller, and the only callers in the inspected tree are fixtures. It requires a frozen ordinary-cycle assignment, a current independently reread Company/configuration, a stored exact mapping fact and one matching qualification. Both tracked descriptors are empty. Injecting `capability='qualified'`, a worker-authored JSON fact or a synthetic qualified coordinator outcome skips this seam.
