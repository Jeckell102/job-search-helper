## Cap trust and acceptance

The legacy TemporaryRetrySink returns the cap acknowledgement based on presence of temporary_retry_outcome_id. A successor-shaped request missing that field can otherwise fall through to enqueue. Reserve the temporary-retry: request namespace at the sink and require exact parent-ID agreement before **any** enqueue path. For selected successors, verify the saved ledger and terminal receipt against the delivered immutable outcome, or simply leave this sink uninvoked and return the verified local cap disposition. A transport acknowledgement comes only from OutcomeDelivery for that exact record. Keep the public writer/ordinary outcome producer from minting a second classification for a selected-owned request or cycle, including a caller omitting the parent field. Preserve ordinary unselected callers. The selected scheduler already refuses a successor as an original retained event.
