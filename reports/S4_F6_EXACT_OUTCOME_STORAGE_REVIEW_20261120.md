## Smallest safe path with historical work retained

The bridge must produce one real, eligible, current and disjoint Company event, with a durable outbox event, bridge receipt/delivery and exact request digest. Ordinary company-only admission keeps it queued while the historical cycle is the effective blocker. Do not fall back to a full start, supersede the historical cycle, run global sync, or use named recovery against that cycle. Named recovery can publish an already-admitted, independent exact plan, but cannot supply the missing admission. Samtec's previously approved membership-only operator path does not transfer to a new Company event. Use a reviewed event-specific retained admission and the existing private exact publisher for the historical-blocked live proof. Finally, v1 preflight fetches before creating its durable exclusive start hold. The event path needs a single durable provider claim and frozen result before it is safe to replay across an interrupted fetch. Keep general dispatch off. Bind an external, exact approval to the current historical witnesses, disjoint Company/source/provider, bridge request and receipt, target workbook/profile and runtime.
