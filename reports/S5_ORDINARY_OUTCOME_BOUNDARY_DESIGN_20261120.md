A proven terminal storage failure outranks complete or verified zero. Complete and verified zero outrank policy/access and temporary failure. Only otherwise unknown incomplete evidence may take the mapping path, with a positive exact verifier result, current source and qualification, and exact verified publication before fact creation and seal. A durably verified negative can use the ordinary classifier. Unresolved capture, uncertain receipt or publication still in progress stays pending. The producer translates `storage_confirmed=False` into `sync_pending`, which is terminal to the existing classifier, so keep that call out of the enrolled pending branch.
