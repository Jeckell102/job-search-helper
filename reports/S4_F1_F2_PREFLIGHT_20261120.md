Last deployed checks had sync and automatic intake off, with queue, campaign, autopilot and automatic release held. The latest restart card still gives the coordinator the Sprint 3 pilot. 2. **Exercise two Company revisions through the real F1 path.** Prove supported mutation atomicity and external first-read bootstrap/catch-up in fixtures. The named SQLite mutation/import APIs had test callers in the inspected tree. Sheets is the existing external entry point for the guarded eligible add while idle and source change while a scoped worker is genuinely busy. Capture source revision → event ID → canonical request digest → delivery/watermark → one admitted Company and worker. Replay after producer and consumer crashes and inject duplicate/reordered delivery in fixtures. In the busy case there is one current worker and one next request, with no fetch before the terminal safe boundary. Record queue age and actual start time. A stored historical `In Progress` row does not provide busy-worker evidence.
3. **Take that event through normal dispatch and one complete outcome ledger.** Use the same pinned build for argument-checked scoped and full-run fixtures. Cover complete and verified zero, one bounded transient retry, access block, one qualified held repair and duplicate, unsupported engineering hold, storage/publish failure with same-plan recovery, and stale/paused/removed with no repair delivery. Verify incomplete inventory preserves unseen jobs and the complete-check timestamp. Join the exact outcome, sender attempt/ack, receiver receipt and repair evidence IDs, with lost-ack replay and no second logical action. Live evidence needs one authorized event-to-outcome-to-action chain. Destructive errors and restart fault injection belong in fixtures.
4. **Prove F2 lineage without opening Sprint 5 policy.** With a reviewed release in a disposable rehearsal, stop after activation, restart into the same intent, and admit a fresh pinned retry through ordinary start. Compare predecessor inventory/plan/cursor/receipt before and after, recover a partial retry publication without refetch, and replay a late original, duplicate, conflicting pin, source drift and rolled-back release. Verify terminal successor result and no predecessor reopening. The inspected release controller had a fixture caller. A live sequence needs a reviewed operational caller, capability and release authority. Under current gates, the live qualified branch stops at the held repair key unless the coordinator can authorize that next boundary.
