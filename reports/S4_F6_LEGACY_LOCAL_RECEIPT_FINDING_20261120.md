# Sprint 4 storage authority finding

The ordinary local pipeline can report legacy `SYNC_CONFIRMED` without a
Google read. In a disposable fixture, the real
`LocalPipeline._reconcile_receipt` read exclusively from an initialized
SQLite repository. One exact Companies record matched the frozen plan. The
actual InventoryInbox then stored `SYNC_CONFIRMED` with a legacy
`readback:` acknowledgement. No Google client existed in the fixture.

That acknowledgement can prove local application on the legacy SQLite
path. It does not prove Sheets publication. The runner's global pending-cell
count is also insufficient to bind a Company request and revision to its
own verified record set. Neither signal may support the new event outcome's
confirmed remote storage status.

Keep the ordinary scraper and its saved legacy format compatible. For
new outcome production, distinguish a proved local result, pending or
failed delivery, and an exact remote receipt tied to the event's request,
Company, source revision, runtime/release and record fingerprints. Reuse
the exact named publication or retained publication authority where its
admission scope fits. Any unsupported publication form must leave honest
pending evidence and a recovery route that preserves the original
inventory. The live Samtec candidate already uses the separate exact
retained publisher and is unaffected.

Independent review owns mapping the trusted publication authorities and
their dependencies into the Sprint 4 intake/outcome path. No production
database or remote workbook was used for this reproduction.
