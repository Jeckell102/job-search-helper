In a disposable composed fixture I kept the staged event's real `company_event_id`, Company and source revision, changed the request to an opaque ordinary alias and used a fabricated ordinary cycle. Add a regression in which the opaque alias is rejected before recording or delivery and the signed route still produces exactly one outcome for the event.
