# Sprint 5 ordinary Rippling capture custody

**Recommendation. Preserve the exact direct HTTP response at the already-reviewed page-zero mismatch branch, and commit it only after the enrolled ordinary dispatch returns the final matching observation.** Keep the public authority and inventory collection in a separate bounded process after the measured worker releases its application leases. The accepted offline prototype remains an offline evidence gate. This design does not enable live collection or qualify a repair.

## 1. Narrow seam in the measured worker

`Transport.request` already keeps the exact response body and final URL from a no-redirect request. `listing` validates the response and only calls `_initial_rippling_tenant_mismatch` for page zero. That helper checks exact configured and final URL, integer HTTP 200, two agreeing foreign tenant fields, the active unfiltered page-zero query, and hashes the response bytes. Put a one-shot **private capture callback immediately after that typed check succeeds on the direct built-in transport**. Do not hook generic HTTP requests. The callback takes the validated observation and the same immutable `response.body`, plus the actual requested/final URL and status. There is no second fetch. Synthetic transports may exercise the callback in tests and cannot issue live capture authority. Live admission must also pin the effective direct HTTP/TLS and proxy policy or hold. The current default opener does not by itself prove that ambient proxy settings were absent. ## 2. Private immutable handoff

Use a persistent host-owned evidence root outside the checkout and normal backups, and a private slot keyed by the exact `dispatch_sha256` under that root. Bind the root and slot to their opened physical directory identities. Use mode 0700 directories and 0600 single-link regular files, no symlinks, descriptor-relative operations, a private per-slot lock, and no writes through caller-supplied paths. For deployment, the host owner must choose and enroll this persistent root. The accepted prototype currently insists on `/tmp`; adapt its reader to a launcher-proven persistent directory descriptor before live operation. Temporary working copies may remain private `/tmp` inputs after readback of the durable originals. A private filesystem and hashes protect custody under an authorized owner. They do not authenticate records planted by a hostile same-UID writer. The launcher and finisher must know and verify the same owned channel. [Prototype boundaries](/tmp/jsh-s5-ordinary-verifier-handoff.md)

1. Before the one owned dispatch, while the exact ARMED snapshot is valid, durably reserve its generation in the private slot. Derive the key from the existing ARMED dispatch digest, which already binds the owner and admission. Preserve the measured runtime/source binding and collector/parser version. The reservation grants only one candidate capture for that dispatch. It gives no authority to write a fact or outcome.
2. After final observation selection, compute the existing F6 payload once and bind its unchanged digest. Stage `configured-response.html` and a small closed manifest. Include dispatch digest, observation generation, measured runtime/source and collector policy digests, exact observation/response digest, source/request/final URL and GET/page-zero/status 200, allowlisted response metadata, final inventory digest, and content length plus SHA-256. No cookies, authorization, browser/session identifiers or foreign listing text go in the manifest. Rehash the file, fsync the files and staging directory, publish the complete pair via a no-replace atomic rename in the private owner directory, and fsync the parent. Reopen and verify before acknowledging custody. Do the raw write before `handoff.save`, outside the writer section. A different result for the same slot is a conflict, not an overwrite.
3. Use the existing unchanged `prepare_inventory` and paired readback under the short writer admission. On export, authoritative `PREPARED` State, current owners, exact matching Inbox payload and the committed private manifest must all agree. Bind the exact serialized `verifier_payload` SHA-256 and capture manifest hash into a separately reviewed version of the private job pin. The accepted offline job and receipt schemas remain unchanged. Existing canonical `dispatch_sha256`, `inventory_sha256` and the typed response hash bind the two stores and the separate bytes without adding raw fields to F6 or to the public receipt. The launcher must reopen/compare exact files before constructing the verifier inputs and close application handles before starting capture or replay. ## 3. Independent public acquisition and live-use gates

Use a dedicated, authorized collector outside both application leases with an exact allowlist for the two company careers pages, PDW's one captured same-host alias, canonical Forterra/PDW listings, and listing-derived canonical detail UUIDs. Never expand the foreign tenant. Preserve origin, requested/final URL, method, response/status and header multiplicity, UTC timestamps, cache freshness evidence, exact entity-body bytes and SHA-256, plus an ordered request ledger. Normal `Transport` and `CareersTransport` flatten response headers into dictionaries and have internal retries or multiple connection attempts. A live collector must count actual attempts before network use, preserve duplicate `Location`/`Refresh` evidence, and bound reads as they happen. Use ordered status/header evidence from the approved client, or an explicitly reviewed structured authority representation. Do not reconstruct a supposed original HTTP transcript from a flattened header map. Keep an exact TLS/egress policy, no credentials or ambient proxy, no automatic redirects, and the existing strict Forterra widget and PDW alias checks. [Normal provider read and retry](/tmp/jsh-s5-ordinary-worker.akf3gPsM/src/job_search_helper/shared_boards.py:307) · [Careers transport](/tmp/jsh-s5-ordinary-worker.akf3gPsM/src/job_search_helper/discovery.py:65) · [Authority rules and prior accepted offline specimen](/tmp/jsh-m5-live-authority-repair-handoff.md)

Reserve a single absolute deadline and bounded live request/byte allowances before collection, and pass the same expiry to the offline replay. The current prototype allows 120 seconds, 128 logical reads and a 64 MiB capsule. Treat those as the initial ceiling, with the configured observation charged once and each public attempt charged before use. Live attempts and offline replay reads get separate counters. Offline reads are not evidence of network attempts. Demonstrate the collector fits the approved deadline in a bounded rehearsal. The earlier manual capture is not evidence that a live run finishes within 120 seconds. Enforce per-body and aggregate limits before accepting the capsule, including any derived JSON expansion. An interruption, oversize response, access denial, freshness beyond the reviewed window, missing detail, listing drift without bounded reconciliation, ambiguous board or unreadable response remains a hold. Commit the capsule atomically with exact replay pin and provenance manifest. The collector never decides release qualification. Bind the current evidence to the reference reader, original mismatch, frozen owner, and exact observation generation. Give live-acquired provenance its reviewed manifest and loader/receipt contract. Do not promote the accepted `offline_saved_captures` result by relabeling it.

After a candidate is produced, independently prove fresh normal dispatch on the exact generated source/configuration/dependencies, with unchanged Company revision, current public authority and PDW control.
