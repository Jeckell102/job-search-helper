**Full check.** Capture a physical inventory before the entire multi-database scan and after closing its last reader, with the same writer fences verified at both ends and effective throughout. **Placement.** Full check before first source mutation; again immediately before publication and before the first reopening that admits any supported writer. Apply the same rules to rollback. **Durability and recovery.** Store a private, versioned checkpoint record outside the managed paths, bound to maintenance ID, target, manifest, policy/helper digest, expected pin digest, inventory digest and journal sequence. Re-establish holds and perform full verification against the original approved pin. Recheck whether the durable fences actually bridged the gap. Keep prior lock inodes, pins and receipt histories intact. A timeout produces no successful receipt or transition. A crash after successful verification but before durable advancement repeats the check.

Terminate and reap the verifier and its descendants on timeout and retain all holds. Allow one explicit recovery recheck per failed boundary with a recorded cause; no automatic retry loop. Measure a representative disposable dataset and validate cold held timing at authorized enrollment before any mutation. An inadequate budget leaves installation pending.
