# Sprint 5 owner-bound offline verifier launch independent QA

**HOLD on the initial launch pin.** The intended three static refusals worked, but an assigned job could lose its token requirement when its in-slot owner manifest was absent. The supervisor also consumed a reservation if the sibling owner record disappeared between its initial check and the first reservation. Both findings occurred in disposable fixtures, with a stub preventing child launch or replay. This report covers the new launch boundary separately from the [accepted stage/readback recheck](/tmp/jsh-s5-ordinary-verifier-handoff-independent-recheck.md).

1. **Assigned job falls into legacy mode without the in-slot manifest.** Passing the original owner token rejected a byte-identical copy at the same pathname and rejected a missing sibling owner record. Omitting the token also rejected the intact assigned slot. Removing only `owner.json` and omitting the token, however, left the exact assigned pending/job bytes and sibling owner record present, and the supervisor treated it as a standalone legacy job. It wrote a verifier `reservation.json` and reached the child stub. Put the owner-required distinction inside the exact pinned job bytes and enforce it before any mutex or reservation. Keep the existing unbound job format for genuine isolated offline fixtures. [Initial authorization check](/tmp/jsh-s5-bound-verifier-qa-pin.vnGXc5bJ/dev/ordinary_rippling_verifier.py:382) · [Independent case](/tmp/jsh-s5-bound-verifier-qa-probes.Dva6Cruq/test_independent_bound_launch.py:39)
2. **The initial sibling check can precede a lost-owner reservation.** An independent cut removed the sibling record during the last pinned input read, after the supervisor’s early authorization check. It wrote `reservation.json` and reached the child stub. Child-side checks detect a missing owner before evidence access, but run after the allowance is reserved. Retain the fail-fast pre-lock check and repeat exact owner authority under the supervisor mutex immediately before publishing the first reservation. [Initial supervisor check and reservation](/tmp/jsh-s5-bound-verifier-qa-pin.vnGXc5bJ/dev/ordinary_rippling_verifier.py:412) · [Independent cut](/tmp/jsh-s5-bound-verifier-qa-probes.Dva6Cruq/test_independent_bound_launch.py:102)

The frozen candidate carried the exact owner token into the child; a direct child check with an otherwise matching private pipe rejected a missing sibling with zero requests. The verifier still supported the isolated legacy fixture and replayed one stored result without a second child invocation. The ordinary static wrong-inode, missing-owner and token-omission cases did not create a lock, reservation or receipt.
