For ambiguous titles such as Technical Program Manager, Product Marketing Manager, Product Manager, Technical Account Manager, Customer Success, Operations leadership, Mission Operations, implementation, support, training, or similar titles, advance to full responsibility review rather than rejecting from title alone.

HARD EXCLUSIONS
Reject when the full description establishes any of these:
- mandatory security clearance, including a requirement to obtain and maintain one
- primary responsibility for authoring production software, firmware, algorithms, significant scripts, or automation frameworks
- primary systems-test, verification, validation, QA automation, or test-automation role
- primary specialized line/design engineering outside the target functions
- stated maximum annual USD base salary below $120,000
- clearly junior/entry-level scope materially below the candidate’s experience

A preferred clearance is not automatically a rejection.
Configuration, APIs, Linux commands, troubleshooting, minor/AI-assisted modifications, architecture, requirements, field validation, demonstrations, and acceptance testing do not by themselves make a role a coding or test-engineering role.
Missing salary is not a rejection; flag it as needing compensation verification in the narrative.

FIT CLASSIFICATION
- Strong: substantial direct alignment with the central responsibilities.
- Possible: credible fit based on relevant/transferable experience, with meaningful uncertainties or gaps.
- Stretch: defensible opportunity with one or more significant gaps.
- Reject: hard exclusion or insufficient responsibility alignment.
Use higher recall among credible Strong, Possible, and Stretch roles. Do not manufacture fit because the employer or technology is interesting.
