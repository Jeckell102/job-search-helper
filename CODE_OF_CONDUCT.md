# Community Guidelines

People with different backgrounds, experience levels, and job-search situations
are welcome here. These guidelines apply to project issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

- Be respectful, patient, and clear. Make room for questions and first contributions.
- Critique ideas and code constructively. Explain your reasoning and accept good-faith correction.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's identity, contact details,
  application history, or private messages without permission.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized content, personal
  attacks, and repeated unwanted contact.
- Keep participation relevant to the project; do not spam or pressure people for
  unpaid work, employment, or immediate responses.

## Addressing Problems

For a public disruption, ask a maintainer to review the relevant issue or pull
request without repeating harmful content. If the report is sensitive, request
a private contact channel with no identifying details in the public request.
Wait for that channel before sharing private information. This project does not
currently publish a dedicated conduct-reporting email address.

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove content, close or lock a discussion,
or restrict participation based on the severity and repetition of the behavior.
Reports should be handled with care and shared only as needed to address them.
No reporting system can promise absolute confidentiality or an immediate response.
